# Ella Koch

Hi! My name is Ella Koch, and I am a first-year student at Oregon State University double majoring in Data Science and Mathematics. I was drawn to data science because of the way it combines statistics and computer science to make sense of complex datasets. Data plays an important role in nearly every field, and I find it exciting that studying data science can open doors to so many different career paths and industries. I have always enjoyed organizing information and finding ways to make sense of data. In high school, I spent hours creating spreadsheets about different topics. One of my yearly projects was making a spreadsheet of all the classes my classmates planned to take. Later, during the collegiate cross country recruiting process, I created a spreadsheet comparing information from over 100 colleges to help organize my options. Looking back, these projects reflected my interest in data analysis before I even realized it could become an area of study. When I started my first quarter at Oregon State, I was initially pursuing only a Mathematics major. However, I quickly realized how well data science complements my interests and gives my mathematical studies a more specific direction. I am excited to continue developing my skills and exploring the many ways data science can be applied to real-world problems.
